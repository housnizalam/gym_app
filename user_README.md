# Gym App

**Measure your training, not just your check-ins.**

Gym App helps you build workout days, track what you actually did, and understand your real effort.
Its core focus is training density, so you can see how hard you trained, not only how often.

---

## What This App Really Does

Gym App is your personal training organizer and intensity tracker.
You can create workout days, add exercises, edit sets, and save favorite routines.
Then the app calculates your training density to show how much load your body handled.

---

## Core Concept: Trainingsdichte (Training Density)

This app is built around one key idea: **Trainingsdichte**.

In simple words, training density tells you how much work you did in a session.
It is based on:

**Sets x Reps x Weight**

Instead of guessing if a workout was "good", you get a clear number you can compare over time.

---

## Why Training Density Is Useful

- It shows your true workout load in one clear value.
- It helps you spot progress even when motivation is low.
- It makes it easier to compare days, weeks, and routines.
- It helps you train smarter, not just harder.
- It gives you confidence that your plan is moving forward.

---

## Main Features

- **Plan Training Days**
	Build a full day with multiple exercises and sets.

- **Use Saved Workout Templates**
	Save a day once, then reuse it anytime with one tap.

- **Create Custom Exercises**
	Add your own exercises and choose targeted muscles.

- **Edit Sets Quickly**
	Update reps and weight per set directly inside the workout flow.

- **See Density by Exercise and Day**
	View total density and muscle-based density breakdown in clear tables.

- **Calendar-Based Training History**
	Pick any date to view what you trained and how dense that session was.

- **Personal Statistics Overview**
	See first training date, last training date, total days, total workouts, daily average, and your best density day.

---

## How To Use

1. Open the app and go to your training area.
2. Choose one path:
	 - start from today,
	 - load a saved workout day,
	 - or create a new workout.
3. Add exercises, then set reps and weight for each set.
4. Save the day or save it as a reusable template.
5. Open the calendar and statistics views to track your density progress.

---

## Progress and Statistics

Gym App helps you track progress in two ways:

- **Session level**: You see exercise density and total day density.
- **Muscle level**: You see a muscle-by-muscle density table.
- **Long-term level**: You see your best training day, total workout count, and training consistency.

This makes progress visible, measurable, and easier to improve.
